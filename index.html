UNIPRO

UNICAP Production Allocation and Ending Inventory Workpaper Tool

Background

UNIPRO computes additional section 263A costs allocable to ending inventory under the simplified production method and modified simplified production method without a historic absorption ratio election.

The application uses account-level amounts and the classifications selected by the user. The application calculates the simplified production method and modified simplified production method from the completed account and inventory detail.

This version applies a FIFO workflow and treats raw materials as one aggregate FIFO pool. It does not determine whether section 263A applies, whether a method may be used, whether an election is effective, or whether consent is required under section 446(e).

Tool ownership, confidentiality, use restrictions, and creator information are provided in Tool Information.

Data are not retained after the page is closed.

Information Needed

Obtain the following before completing the workpaper:

  • The selected tax-basis or book-basis trial balance with current-year costs and separate beginning and ending inventory balances.
  • Beginning raw materials. Beginning resale inventory, work in process, finished goods, and the beginning additional Section 263A layer may be included as supporting information.
  • Current-year raw-material purchases, resale purchases, Section 471 production costs, and additional Section 263A costs.
  • Ending raw materials and the current-year resale and production costs remaining on hand.
  • Detailed support for summarized COGS, purchases, production costs, inventory variances, reserves, write-downs, and book-tax adjustments.
  • The taxpayer's Section 471 capitalization method, existing UNICAP method, mixed service cost method, and producer de minimis rule determination.
  • All current-year labor and trade or business cost accounts used in the mixed service allocation ratio.
